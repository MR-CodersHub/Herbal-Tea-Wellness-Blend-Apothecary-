The Founder's Story

A Garden, A Dream,
& Eight Years Later

It began in 2018 in Dr. Elena Voss's grandmother's garden in Bavaria. Surrounded by chamomile, lavender, and rose hips, Elena — a trained phytochemist — saw how her grandmother's simple herbal remedies outperformed many pharmaceuticals she'd studied.

Returning to California, she set up a small laboratory in her kitchen, determined to create formulations backed by both traditional wisdom and clinical research. What started as gifts for friends became Verdana Apothecary — a brand that now serves 50,000+ customers across 42 countries.

"I want every cup to feel like a letter from nature," Elena says. "A gentle reminder that the earth provides everything we need to thrive."

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To make premium, evidence-based herbal wellness accessible to everyone — bridging ancient botanical traditions with modern scientific validation, one cup at a time.

Our Vision

A world where botanical medicine is the first choice for wellness — not the last resort. Where communities thrive through nature, and our planet heals through sustainable herbal practices.

Sustainability

Carbon-neutral operations, compostable packaging, and reforestation partnerships for every order placed.

Evidence-Based

Every formula is backed by peer-reviewed research. We cite our sources and never make unfounded health claims.

Fair Trade

We pay above-market rates to our farmers, invest in community development, and maintain long-term supplier relationships.

Uncompromising Quality

We reject up to 40% of sourced botanicals that don't meet our strict potency and purity standards. Quality is never negotiable.
